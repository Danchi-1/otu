Why VIO exists

Make it easier to be heard — and safer to listen.

VIO is being built as a structured channel for students to raise academic concerns and for institutions to see aggregate patterns without turning individual reports into public accusations.

The idea

From isolated concern to responsible signal.

Academic concerns can be difficult to raise, hard to follow, and easy to misunderstand. VIO aims to give students a clearer process: describe what happened, understand that reports are claims, and provide a careful route for review.

The public experience is intentionally different from a complaint feed. It is designed to surface only published aggregate patterns, with privacy protections that prevent a reader from drilling down to people or raw reports.

The boundary

Technology supports process.

Automated checks may help assess consistency and safety, but model output is not presented as a verdict. The product should explain what is known, what is still being reviewed, and what the next step is.
