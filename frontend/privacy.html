Privacy & responsible reporting

A voice platform should protect the people using it.

These are the product boundaries VIO is designed to follow. This preview is not a substitute for a finalized privacy notice or legal advice.

Identity should stay private

Student email is intended to verify eligibility. The backend stores a keyed identity hash rather than the raw email; the frontend must never put email addresses, account identifiers, or session tokens in public pages, URLs, analytics, or client logs.

A report is a claim

A report must not be presented as a verified fact or public accusation. The UI should display statuses supplied by the backend and use neutral language while a claim is being assessed.

Public information is aggregate-only

The public dashboard must not expose report text, tracking codes, student identities, lecturer names, or individual supporter details. Groups smaller than the approved minimum of 20 students should be suppressed.

This browser preview

The sample report interaction runs only in your browser. It sends nothing to the API, does not persist entered content, and does not issue a real tracking code. Please do not enter real personal or sensitive information.
