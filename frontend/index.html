Trust is part of the product

Designed with care.

Raising a concern can feel difficult. VIO’s product principles put privacy, context, and careful language first.

01

Identity stays private

School email is intended to verify student eligibility, not appear on public reports.

02

A claim is not a finding

One report does not establish that an issue occurred or justify a public accusation.

03

Public means aggregate

The intended public experience excludes names, report text, and individual tracking codes.
